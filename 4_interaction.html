Duluth Public School Attendance Explorer

By: Sydney Ford

This interactive map displays the attendance boundaries for Duluth Public Schools, with each area shown in a different color. School locations are marked with custom icons, and clicking a school highlights the attendance area it serves. You can also click any attendance zone to see the elementary, middle, and high schools assigned to that part of the city. This map provides a clear, easy‑to‑use way to explore how Duluth’s school district is organized.
