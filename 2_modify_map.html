My Favorite Places in Duluth

by: Sydney Ford

This map shows some of my favorite places in Duluth, Minnesota. Each spot that I love has a marker on the map, and when you click on the marker, you will get information about that place. There is a wide variety of places on this map, including Dovetail Cafe & Marketplace, Glensheen Mansion, and Park Point. I hope you enjoy learning about these places as much as I do!
